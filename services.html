Good to Know

FAQ

Do you come to my location?

Yes — we offer mobile detailing throughout San Francisco and Daly City. We just need access to water and power at your location, or we can bring our own supply for an added fee.

How long does a full detail take?

As a two-person team, a Full Detail typically takes 2–3.5 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. Deep Clean packages can take longer.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Cash, Zelle, Venmo, and major credit/debit cards. Payment is collected after the job is complete and you're happy with the result.

How do I get an exact quote?

Submit the booking form with your vehicle details, or call/text us at (415) 361-3549 — we'll confirm final pricing before we start.

Not sure which package fits your car?

Give us a call and we'll help you pick the right detail.
